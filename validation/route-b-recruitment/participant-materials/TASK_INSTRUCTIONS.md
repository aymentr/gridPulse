# Task Instructions

You will complete two tasks, one for each document set. Your facilitator will tell you which set to
use for each task, and in which order.

All documents are **synthetic**. They describe a fictional project and contain no real project,
company or confidential information.

## The question (both tasks)

> **What changed, what may it affect, what evidence supports that, and who needs to look at it?**

Work as you normally would. Take notes in any form. When your answer to the question is complete, tell
the facilitator. Timing ends at that point.

## Task type 1 — documents only

You receive one document set. Investigate it and answer the question.

## Task type 2 — documents plus an investigation bundle

You receive the other document set **and** a pre-prepared investigation bundle. The bundle was prepared
manually in the style of the GridPulse prototype; it is not live software output.

Review the bundle against the documents. Accept, reject or correct its items, add anything it misses,
and give your answer to the same question. Everything you do in this task counts toward the task time,
including reading and checking the bundle.

## Please note

- There is no expected answer you need to agree with. Disagreeing with the bundle is as useful as
  agreeing with it.
- This is not a test of your professional ability. It evaluates an investigation workflow.
- Please do not search for outside information about the documents; they are fictional.
