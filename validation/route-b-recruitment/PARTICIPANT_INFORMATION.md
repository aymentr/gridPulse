# Participant Information

Thank you for considering taking part.

## What this is

A controlled test of an investigation workflow for infrastructure-project information, run as part of
developing a research prototype called GridPulse. It is not a sales demonstration, and taking part does
not mean endorsing GridPulse or any product.

## What you will do

You will investigate two synthetic infrastructure-project document sets, answering the same question
for each: *what changed, what may it affect, what evidence supports that, and who needs to look at it?*

- For one set, you work from the source documents only.
- For the other set, you also receive a pre-prepared investigation bundle written in the style of the
  GridPulse prototype, and you review and correct it.

Which set comes first, and which set comes with the bundle, is rotated between participants according
to the experimental design. The bundle was prepared manually for this test; it is not live software
output.

**Session length: approximately 90 minutes.** The session is expected to take approximately 90
minutes in total: a short introduction and instructions, the first task, a short transition, the second
task, and a brief wrap-up. The actual investigation times are recorded as part of the study and are not
expected to match a fixed target. There is no time limit for either task; if you need more or less
time, that is fine.

## Important disclosure

- The document sets are **synthetic**. They describe a fictional project.
- The documents have **not** been independently reviewed for technical realism. You may notice details
  that differ from your own experience; you are welcome to mention them.
- No confidential information about your employer or your projects is requested. Please do not share
  any.
- Participation is voluntary and you may stop at any time.
- No payment is offered.

## What is recorded

- the time each task takes
- the findings you report and the evidence you cite
- for the bundle task, which bundle items you accept, reject or correct, and what you add
- whether any stated conclusion goes beyond what the documents support
- any comments you choose to make

## What is not being evaluated

Your professional competence is not being scored. The test evaluates an investigation workflow. There
is no right answer you are expected to reach, and disagreeing with the bundle is useful information.

## Your data

Records use a participant code, your professional role and your relevant experience only — no name,
employer or project names unless you choose to share them and consent to it being recorded.
