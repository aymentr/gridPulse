# Route A / Route B Separation

> Internal.

Route A (practitioner interviews, `docs/VALIDATION_PLAN.md` §5) is a separate real-world problem and
buyer validation stream. Route B participants are **not** automatically Route A interviewees.

- **Route B can proceed independently** of Route A.
- **Route A evidence remains pending.** Status: NOT STARTED. No interviews are recorded in
  `validation/interview/`.
- **No Route A interviews may be fabricated**, including by re-labelling Route B sessions or
  participant comments as interviews.
- **Route B results must not be used as evidence of** willingness to pay, market demand, buyer
  identity or problem frequency.
- A Route B participant may separately agree to a Route A interview. If so, it is a distinct session
  recorded with `docs/PRACTITIONER_INTERVIEW_TEMPLATE.md`, and the interview must not discuss the
  Route B documents, ground truth or results.
- The gate report (`VALIDATION_PLAN.md` §11) includes Route A findings and the §10 stop/continue
  conditions. Missing Route A evidence is stated as missing; it is never treated as supportive
  (`validation/ROUTE_B_EXECUTION_CHECKLIST.md` §L).
