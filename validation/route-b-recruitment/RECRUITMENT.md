## Participant profile (from `docs/VALIDATION_PLAN.md` §6 and the Route B checklist)

Practitioners who investigate changes across multiple project documents, with experience in one or
more of: Owner's Engineering · technical project control · BESS engineering · electrical/grid
engineering · commissioning · HV/MV infrastructure · BESS EPC technical management · infrastructure
project technical coordination. Germany / DACH preferred. Experience with GridPulse is not required.

**Exclude:** anyone who created the benchmark or bundles; anyone who has seen the ground truth or
bundles; anyone who has reviewed the packs as a technical-realism reviewer.

Route B participants are experiment participants only — not technical reviewers, customer
interviewees, product advisors, validators of the scenarios, or sources of market evidence. See
`ROUTE_A_SEPARATION.md`.

## Target number

The validation plan does **not** define a Route B participant number (the "8–12" in §5 refers to
Route A interviews). The crossover design (D-048) rotates participants through four groups (G1–G4); one
complete rotation needs four participants. That is a property of the design, not a statistical
requirement, and no sample here is representative of any population.
