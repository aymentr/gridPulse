## 1. Purpose

This plan defines the phases, preconditions and responsibilities for commissioning the Kestrel Moor
BESS facility up to and including support for energization and grid compliance testing.

## 2. Commissioning phases

| Phase | Description |
|---|---|
| P1 | Pre-commissioning (mechanical completion checks, cold checks) |
| P2 | LV/MV commissioning — battery containers, PCS skids, 33 kV system |
| P3 | HV commissioning — 132 kV system |
| P4 | Support to grid compliance testing and energization |

## 3. Equipment classification

Equipment is classified LV, MV or HV in accordance with the Equipment List KMB-EPC-EQL-001. The
classification in the Equipment List governs which commissioning phase applies.

## 4. LV/MV commissioning (P2)

4.1 LV/MV commissioning of each battery block may proceed once the associated containers and PCS skid
are installed and DC/AC cabling is terminated.

4.2 33 kV switchgear functional testing shall be completed before any 33 kV circuit is energized.

## 5. HV commissioning (P3)

5.1 **Preconditions.** HV commissioning shall commence once all HV equipment is installed, terminated
and has passed pre-commissioning checks, and the substation control building is complete.

5.2 **Activities.** Insulation resistance and high-voltage withstand tests as applicable; CT and VT
tests; secondary injection testing of protection relays; functional testing of HV switchgear
interlocks, trip and close circuits; verification of protection settings against the approved
protection study.

5.3 **Duration.** Approximately six working days.

## 6. Support to grid compliance testing (P4)

Stage 1 grid compliance tests shall not commence until HV commissioning (P3) is complete. See
KMB-EPC-GCT-PRG-001.

## 7. Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Commissioning Manager (EPC) | Overall commissioning execution |
| Electrical Lead Engineer (EPC) | HV commissioning technical lead |
| Owner's Engineer | Witnessing and review of commissioning records |
