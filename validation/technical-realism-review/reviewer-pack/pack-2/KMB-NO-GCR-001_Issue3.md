## Section R — Technical requirements (extract)

**R-10 Voltage control.** The facility shall be capable of controlling voltage at the point of
connection (POC) within the range specified by the Network Operator.

**R-12 Reactive power capability.** The facility shall be capable of operating at any power factor
between 0.95 leading and 0.95 lagging at the POC across the full active power range from 0 % to 100 %
of Registered Capacity, for both import and export.

**R-14 Fault ride-through.** The facility shall remain connected during and after voltage dips as
specified in Appendix R2.

**R-18 Frequency response.** The facility shall provide frequency response as specified in the
Connection Agreement.

**R-20 Compliance testing.** The connecting party shall demonstrate compliance with R-10 to R-18
through tests witnessed by the Network Operator, in stages before and after energization, according to
a test plan agreed with the Network Operator.

**R-30 Notification of changes.** The connecting party shall notify the Network Operator of any
change to plant equipment or control systems that may affect compliance with these requirements,
before compliance testing of the affected capability.

**R-31 Simulation models.** Where a change to plant or control systems affects the dynamic
performance of the facility, the connecting party shall submit updated simulation models to the
Network Operator.
